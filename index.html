About

who is bronoway?

I'm Tharusha Nethman, a 21 year old undergraduate majoring in Software Engineering at SLIIT - Malabe, with a genuine love for building things that look as good as they work. Outside coursework, I run a creative practice spanning video editing, graphic design, and web development — which shapes how I approach every project: with an eye for detail and a bias toward clean, purposeful design. I'm always looking for the next thing worth building.
